Position Summary
As an Associate Engineer – Implementation, you'll work alongside experienced
implementation engineers to deploy, test, and support GreyOrange's AI-powered warehouse
automation solutions.
This role combines software engineering, systems integration, customer interaction, and
problem-solving. You'll help ensure our software integrates seamlessly with Warehouse
Management Systems (WMS), robotic fleets, and customer operations.
This is an excellent opportunity for a recent graduate interested in cloud technologies, robotics,
automation, supply chain technology, and enterprise software.
